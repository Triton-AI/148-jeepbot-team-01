## 1. Overview

The mechanical system of the robot consists of custom-designed mounts used to securely attach sensors such as cameras and LiDAR to the jeep platform.

These mounts ensure:

- Stable sensor positioning  
- Consistent data collection  
- Proper alignment for perception systems  

Detailed CAD models, component breakdowns, and step-by-step installation instructions are provided in the full mounts documentation.

## 3. Mounts Included in the System

### Front Camera Mount
- Holds the forward-facing OAK-D camera  
- Aligns with vehicle driving direction  

### Rear Camera Mounts (Two Configurations)

**Bar Mount**
- Attaches camera to structural bar  
- Uses clamp-based support  

**Ridge Mount**
- Positions camera higher on trunk  
- Improves rear perception  

### LiDAR Mount
- Secures LiDAR on top of vehicle  
- Enables 360° environmental scanning  

---

## 4. Mount Design Considerations

All mounts are designed to:

- Maintain rigid attachment during motion  
- Minimize vibration  
- Ensure correct orientation and field of view  
- Allow access for wiring and connections  

---

## 5. Installation Notes

- Follow the full documentation carefully  
- Some mounts may require cutting or drilling  
- Proper alignment is critical  
- Multiple iterations may be needed for best fit
